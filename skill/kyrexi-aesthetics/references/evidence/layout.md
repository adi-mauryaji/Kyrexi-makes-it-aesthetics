# Evidence · Layout, order and complexity (LAY)

### LAY-001 · Make complexity orderly: order and complexity each shape liking; ordered + simple feels soothing, ordered + rich feels fascinating
- grade: B
- applies: deciding density per section and per audience mood (calm audiences: ordered and simple; curious audiences: ordered and rich)
- not-for: —
- value: whatever the amount of content, group and align it so it reads as ordered; choose "ordered and simple" for calm, anxious or older audiences, "ordered and rich" where exploration is the point
- source: Van Geert & Wagemans, Order, Complexity, and Aesthetic Preferences for Neatly Organized Compositions · author manuscript, KU Leuven Lirias · https://lirias.kuleuven.be/retrieve/552891 · access: OA · reuse: ©
- verified: 2026-11-13 full text (manuscript)
- caveats: images of neatly arranged objects, not web pages; 421 online participants aged 16–77 (mean 39.8) made 100 paired choices, 84 also rated 184 images. Subjective order and subjective complexity were related to appreciation independently. Journal and year not confirmed from the manuscript.
