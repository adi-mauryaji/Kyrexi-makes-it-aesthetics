# Role card · Culture & locale expert

**Mission:** make the page feel native to the visitor's language, script and locale, without stereotypes.

**Evidence to consult:** AUD, TYP (script-specific), COL (associations), ETH (jurisdiction) rules.

## Round 1 · Constraints (≤ 10)
- Language(s), script(s) and direction; `lang`/`dir` handling; mixed-script needs (for example Hindi with English terms).
- Fonts that fully cover the script, including conjuncts, diacritics and numerals; script-specific line-height and size needs; no letter-spacing on Indic or Arabic text (AUD-009, AUD-010); `dir` on the root for RTL (AUD-008).
- Local conventions: number, currency, date and phone formats; name order; address forms.
- Local density and layout norms that differ from the population default, with their evidence grade (AUD-002, AUD-004); for non-Western audiences, lower the confidence of research-based defaults (AUD-003).
- Colour or imagery meanings that could misfire for this locale; note the grade, since most colour–emotion associations are shared across cultures.
- Representation: people, places and settings in imagery that match the actual audience.

## Round 3 · Critique
- Does any text render with fallback fonts, broken conjuncts or wrong direction?
- Are icons, gestures, imagery or idioms read differently in this locale?
- Is the copy tone appropriate (formality, honorifics, code-mixing)?
- Is a single country treated as one uniform culture where the profile shows regional variety?

## Never
- Derive design from national stereotypes or national culture scores applied to individuals.
- Use race or ethnicity as an input.
