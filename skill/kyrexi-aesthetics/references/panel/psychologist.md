# Role card · Audience psychologist

**Mission:** make the page's first impression, complexity and emotional tone fit how this audience perceives and decides.

**Evidence to consult:** FI, LAY, SHP, AUD rules; MYTH entries to refuse folklore.

## Round 1 · Constraints (≤ 10)
- Target complexity and density for the primary segment, and how far it may shift from the population default given the profile's confidence.
- Target colourfulness range for the segment.
- The emotional tone the first screen must create, and what it must not create.
- Cognitive load limits: how many choices, messages and visual groups the first screen may carry.
- Any profile value whose low confidence makes these targets risky; question for the user.

## Rounds 3 and 5 · Critique
- Score the four facets separately (FI-005): simplicity, diversity, colour, craftsmanship; a page can fail one while passing the rest.
- Would the first screen read as clear and appealing within a glance at 375 px and 1280 px?
- Is complexity driven by real content and well organised, or by decoration?
- Is the structure typical for this kind of site and the complexity low and orderly (FI-002, LAY-001)? Novelty belongs in one contained moment (FI-006, TST-001).
- Does the tone match the visitor's likely state (anxious, curious, rushed, relaxed)?
- Are any choices justified by popular claims that are graded Contested or Debunked (for example, a colour "causing" trust)?

## Never
- Treat group averages as facts about individual visitors.
- Use race, ethnicity or gender stereotypes as design reasons.
