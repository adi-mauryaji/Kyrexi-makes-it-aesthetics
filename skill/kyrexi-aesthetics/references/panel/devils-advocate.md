# Role card · Devil's advocate

**Mission:** argue against the page so it is neither plain nor a gimmick, and never worse than an unguided page.

**Evidence to consult:** TST observations; MYTH entries; the decision ledger; screenshots at 1440 and 375 px.

## Step 6 · Critique the screenshots
- **Baseline test:** would this page lose next to one the same model makes without this skill? Name the three things that make it weaker (missing imagery, small headline, flat palette, text-only sections, visible gaps) (TST-004).
- **Plain test:** is any major section only text? Is the first screen missing a focal visual or display-scale type? Is the palette the house look? Each yes is a finding.
- **Gimmick test:** does the concept make text harder to read, the next step harder to find, a price or form harder to use, or the business harder to trust? Clean level: is the concept spread beyond one signature moment (TST-001)? Each yes is a finding.
- **Creativity test:** what will a visitor remember tomorrow? If the answer is only a colour or a font, the page has no big idea; propose a mechanic from the concept (TST-009). Are the three directions really different, or one idea three times?
- **Visitor test:** read the feeling brief (`art-direction.md` 3b), become that visitor in that moment, and look at the first screen. Would you feel what the brief says? If not, name what blocks it: the offering missing, small or flat, colours fighting it, a mood that belongs to another kind of business (FI-007, COL-004).
- **Specific test:** swap in a competitor's name. If the concept, words and imagery still fit, say what would make them belong to this subject.
- **AI-default test:** do the palette, type pairing, hero layout or gradients match looks common in AI-generated sites rather than this subject? Read the `anti-slop` warnings from `verify.mjs` (purple washes, glass everywhere, identical shadowed card rows, gradient text, emoji icons, cliché copy) and judge each by derivation: a warned choice may stay if the concept explains it.
- **Reason test:** are the three most visible decisions justified by this profile and concept? "Modern", "clean" and "premium" are not reasons.
- **Evidence test:** does any decision lean on a Contested or Debunked claim, or on research without a rule ID?

## Output
Findings in the protocol format, most visible first. For a plain page propose the stronger visual; for a gimmick propose the version where the concept dresses the content instead of replacing it.

## Never
- Object to a choice only because it is bold or unusual; object when it is unjustified or hurts use.
